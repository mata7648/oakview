About the Property

A well-maintained 4-bedroom home on a quiet residential street in Saanich. The main floor features a spacious open-plan kitchen with island, a comfortable living room, and a dining area with access to the back deck and yard.

The property backs onto a green space, offering a private and peaceful outdoor area. It's a friendly, tidy household — currently home to UVic students — just walking distance to the Saanich Recreation Centre, close to parks, and with easy transit to UVic.

Shared amenities include the kitchen, living and dining rooms, in-suite laundry, and WiFi. Two friendly cats are part of the household.
