About This Room

This is a master bedroom for rent in a friendly household in Saanich. The room is upstairs and has its own private bathroom with a hot tub.

The house has 4 bedrooms. The other 3 are occupied by female UVic students, so it's a calm and tidy place to live. Everyone gets along well and respects each other's space.

The kitchen, living room, dining room, and laundry are shared. WiFi is included. Utilities like hydro and water are split evenly — around $40/month each. There are two cats in the house. The house is in a great spot — quiet street, walking distance to the Saanich Recreation Centre, close to parks, and easy to get to UVic.
